! Severe bleeding Control blood loss and get urgent help
  1. Apply firm, direct pressure to the wound using a clean cloth or dressing.
  2. If blood soaks through, keep pressure on the wound and add another dressing rather than removing the first.
  3. Call emergency services for severe or uncontrolled bleeding. For life-threatening limb bleeding, use a commercial tourniquet if available and you know how to use it; follow emergency-dispatch instructions.

Important: Do not delay emergency help for severe bleeding.

♡ Unconscious but breathing Protect the airway and monitor breathing
  1. Call emergency services and check that the person is breathing normally.
  2. If there is no suspected spinal injury, place them in the recovery position and keep their airway open.
  3. Keep checking their breathing, keep them warm, and do not give food or drink.
  4. If breathing becomes absent or abnormal, follow the emergency operator's instructions and start CPR if advised and able.

Important: If a serious neck or back injury is suspected, avoid unnecessary movement unless needed to keep the airway open or perform CPR.

✳ Burns Cool the area and protect the skin
  1. Cool the burn under cool or lukewarm running water for 20 minutes as soon as possible.
  2. Remove jewellery and clothing near the burn if it is not stuck to the skin.
  3. Do not use ice, butter, toothpaste, or creams. After cooling, cover loosely with a sterile non-stick dressing or clean material.
  4. Seek urgent medical care for large or deep burns, chemical or electrical burns, or burns affecting the face, hands, feet, or genitals.

Important: Do not pull away material stuck to the burn.
